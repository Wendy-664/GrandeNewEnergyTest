High Bay 100W

FL-YE-01W0100V144V01 — a 100 W UFO high bay on the YE-01 platform, lab-tested with our in-house YT2181GTE5 driver IC. Ship finished luminaires, or knocked-down kits for local assembly in India.

MOQ 300 pcs  ·  Lead time 25 days  ·  FOB Shanghai / Nhava Sheva

  • 4 kV surge passed: 5 counts per polarity at 0 / 90 / 180 / 270° phase

SKD or CKD — same fixture, different local content

The luminaire is deliberately designed for local assembly: no glued driver, plug-in connectors, standard screws.

SKD Kit

Semi-knocked-down. Housing, LED board and driver arrive pre-assembled and tested — your line does final QC, branding and pack-out.

  • Fastest ramp-up, lowest line investment
  • 100% photometric and hipot test before shipment
  • Your logo on housing, label and carton
→ Best for a first container

CKD Kit

Completely-knocked-down. Die-cast body, lens, gasket, LED board, driver board and hardware ship loose for full local assembly.

  • Lowest duty and highest in-country value
  • Driver board and LED board supplied separately for local SMT
  • Assembly manual, jig drawings and test fixtures provided
→ Best for local-content and PLI requirements
