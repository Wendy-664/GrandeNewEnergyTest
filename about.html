Company Profile

Wuxi Grandemicro Technology Co., Ltd. (Grande Micro) is a fabless power semiconductor IC design company headquartered in Wuxi, Jiangsu, China. We deliver core control chips and technical solutions for smart lighting, high-efficiency low-power adapters, wireless power and energy harvesting.

Our founding team comes from Analog Devices, Inc. and Giantec Semiconductor Inc., bringing deep expertise in system integration technology and continuous process improvement. We own multiple invention patents and maintain close partnerships with wafer fabs, packaging houses and test facilities.

For the Indian market, we focus on LED drivers, AC-DC, DC-DC and BLDC controllers that withstand India's wide grid voltage, high surge conditions and cost-sensitive assembly environment.
