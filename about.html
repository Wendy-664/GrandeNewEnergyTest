About Grande New Energy

We are a Wuxi-based LED lighting export company with a chip-design DNA — and it changes what we can promise.

Technology inside, trade outside

Grande New Energy started as a power semiconductor design house. Our driver ICs are our own silicon — designed, verified and continuously improved by our engineers in Wuxi.

Today we put that technology to work as an LED luminaire exporter: street lights, high bays and flood lights, supplied as SKD / CKD kits for local assembly by our partners.

Because we design the driver ourselves, we control the two things that decide whether a luminaire survives in Asian grids: surge immunity and thermal behaviour. Every platform design is verified in our own lab across 100–440 Vac before it ever ships.

What we stand for

Verified, Not Promised

Measured efficiency, PF, THD and thermal data in every test report. If we haven't tested it in our lab, we don't print it on the datasheet.

Partner Profitability

SKD / CKD exists to make our partners' assembly business profitable — duty savings, simple assembly, and a BOM that keeps working at scale.

Long-Term Supply

We own the driver technology, so we never lose access to our core component. Multi-year supply commitments are easy when the silicon is yours.

Where we operate

Our luminaires and kits are engineered and deployed for the region's toughest grid conditions — wide voltage swings, surges and heat.

We are now onboarding assembly and distribution partners in Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia and the Philippines. If you run an assembly line or a lighting distribution business in the region, we should talk.
