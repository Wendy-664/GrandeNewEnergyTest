SKD vs CKD — what you get

Pick the kit that fits your assembly line, duty profile and local-content requirement.

SKD Kit

Semi-knocked-down. Motor and blades pre-assembled — your line does the final fitting, branding and pack-out.

→ Faster to market · moderate duty saving

CKD Kit

Completely-knocked-down. All parts shipped loose for full local assembly — maximum in-country content.

→ Lowest duty · meets local-content rules
