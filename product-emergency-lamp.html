Frequently asked questions

Answers use the same platform data as the specification table above. Where a number has not been measured for this exact wattage, we say so instead of quoting a round figure.

How long does it stay lit after a power cut?

Run time is set by the battery pack against a maximum emergency drive current of 1 A, so it depends on which pack you specify rather than on the lamp alone. Tell us the run time your code requires and we will size the pack to match it.

We do not quote a headline number for this, because a run time without a stated load and battery capacity is not a specification — it is a guess dressed up as one.

How quickly does the pack recharge?

The charging circuit is designed for short daily grid windows, so a brief period of mains power is enough to keep the pack ready. That suits markets where the supply is intermittent rather than off for a full day.

Recharge time depends on pack capacity and on how deeply it was discharged, so confirm it against your duty cycle before quoting a figure to a customer.

Is the battery pack included in the kit?

Yes, when specified — “battery pack included in kit” is an option on this model. Many buyers choose to ship the lamp and the pack separately, which is cheaper on air freight and avoids dangerous-goods handling on the lamp itself.

What protects the pack from being ruined?

Overcharge protection cuts in at about 4.2 V and over-discharge at about 2.4 V. Both matter more than capacity in this product category: most emergency lamps fail early because the pack was left discharged, not because it was too small.

Over-discharge protection is the one to ask about when you compare suppliers — it is often left out to save a few cents.

Can it charge properly on a weak grid?

The input stage charges correctly from 90 to 440 Vac, which covers sagging feeders and night-time overvoltage without damaging the charging circuit. Surge immunity is 4 kV line-to-line, consistent with the rest of the platform.

Efficiency is above 87 % at 230 Vac input, so very little of the charging energy is lost as heat in a closed fitting.

How do I ship a lamp with a lithium pack?

Battery packs are regulated for air transport, so most customers ship lamps and packs separately and consolidate at destination. Tell us the destination and the mode and we will confirm which documents we can provide for that route.

Do not assume a supplier’s generic certificate covers your lane — dangerous-goods rules differ by carrier and by route.
