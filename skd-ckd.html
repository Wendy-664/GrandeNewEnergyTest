SKD / CKD Solutions

We ship luminaires as Semi-Knocked-Down (SKD) or Completely-Knocked-Down (CKD) kits. You import parts, assemble locally, sell as a domestic product.

Why assemble locally?

01

Lower Import Duty

Component kits are classified under lower HS codes than finished luminaires — meaningful duty savings on every container.

02

Certification Made Simple

Local assembly aligns with national certification schemes such as BIS and SIRIM — government tenders become reachable.

03

"Made Locally" Label

Assembled in-country means you can market as a domestic manufacturer — a decisive edge in municipal tenders.

04

Lower Logistics Risk

Flat-packed parts survive rough handling better than finished glass fixtures — less breakage, fewer claims.

What's inside a kit?

Every kit contains everything needed to assemble a complete, working luminaire — down to screws and gaskets. One platform, three product families, one BOM logic.

  • Die-cast aluminum housing & tempered glass
  • LED board with high-efficiency chips
  • Pre-tested driver board (our self-developed platform)
  • Silicone gasket, screws, cable gland, bracket
  • Packing carton with foam protection

Assembly is simpler than you think

No SMT line required. If your team can do screw-driver assembly and basic wiring, they can build our luminaires.

01

Mount LED Board

Fix the LED board to the housing with screws and apply thermal paste — about 2 minutes.

02

Connect Driver

Plug the pre-tested driver board: two wires to the LED board, two to the input cable. Solder or connector, your choice.

03

Seal & Close

Lay the silicone gasket, close the glass cover, torque the perimeter screws evenly.

04

Burn-in Test

Power on for 5–10 minutes, check light output and temperature — done. We provide a test checklist with every first order.

Typical learning curve: one day of training for a new assembly line. Remote support and video guides included.
