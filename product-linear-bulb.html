Frequently asked questions

Answers use the same platform data as the specification table above. Where a number has not been measured for this exact wattage, we say so instead of quoting a round figure.

Why 90–440 Vac when my grid is 220–240 V?

Because the grid you are quoted is not the grid you get. Feeder voltage sags at the end of a line, rises at night when load drops, and swings hard when a large load switches nearby. A lamp rated only for 220–240 V works in the lab and fails in the field.

The wide range also means one SKU covers every market in the region, which simplifies your own stock keeping.

How does one driver BOM cover the whole 9–50 W range?

Over-temperature protection is set by an external resistor rather than being fixed inside the IC. Change that resistor and the same board serves a different wattage, so you buy one driver in volume and configure it per SKU at assembly instead of carrying five part numbers.

Why does THD below 15 % matter in practice?

Because you are not the only load on the transformer. High harmonic distortion from lighting heats shared distribution transformers and neutral conductors, and utilities in several markets now check it at the connection point.

Under 15 % at 230 Vac full load keeps a large lighting installation from becoming the reason a building fails a power quality inspection.

Are the lamp bases included?

Bases are an option, not standard: specify E27 or B22 and they ship with the kit. Most customers assembling locally prefer to source bases in-country, because they are bulky, cheap and easier to buy regionally than to ship from us.

What does 105 lm/W mean for my market?

It is a measured system figure for the series, not a chip datasheet number, so it is the value you can put in front of a utility or a tender specification. Whether it clears a particular energy-efficiency tier depends on the scheme — send us the tier you are targeting and we will confirm which wattages qualify.

Who files the certification in my country — you or me?

You do. BIS, SIRIM, TISI and SNI all require a locally registered applicant. We provide the test data, technical file and driver documentation your lab needs, and the resulting certificate stays in your name rather than ours.
