YT2173E8 in a 9 W B22 bulb — 176–265 Vac linear stage

3-Stage Linear Constant Current LED Driver

Three-stage linear constant-current controller designed for Indian LED bulbs, T-lamps, panels and downlights. Supports parallel connection up to 50W with Iout foldback under low/high line conditions.

  • India grid hardened: wide input range and surge protection
  • Cost-optimized package and BOM for high-volume assemblies
  • Application reference design and samples available
  • Customization support for pinout, OTP and protection thresholds
